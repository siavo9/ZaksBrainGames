How to Play ⚖️✨

Welcome to the Balance Bazaar! The golden star on top shows the whole number. The two pans hold its parts — but one part is missing, so the scale is tipped!

  • ⭐ Look at the number in the golden star — that's the whole
  • 🪙 One pan already has a number tile — that's one part
  • 🤔 Figure out what's missing: the two pans must add up to the star
  • ⌨️ Type your answer and hit Submit (or press Enter)
  • ⚖️ Get it right and watch the scale swing into perfect balance!
  • 😊 Tap Easy Mode for smaller numbers
  • 🔊 Tap the speaker to mute or unmute sounds
