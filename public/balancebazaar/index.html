❓ How to Play

⚖️ This is a part–whole balance scale.

🟦 The two pans each hold a part.

⭐ The golden star on top holds the whole — the two parts added together.

❓ One number is missing! Tap the right number on the brass buttons to balance the scale.

💡 If the star is missing: add the parts. If a pan is missing: think "what's left?"
