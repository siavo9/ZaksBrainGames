🧩 Word Trio

Three themed words, one fun puzzle! Guess letters to solve them all.

How to Play

Word Trio gives you three words that share a fun theme — solve all three to win!

  • Each round shows a theme (like Farm Animals) and three hidden words with clues.
  • Pick a letter by tapping the keyboard, typing in the box, or using your keyboard.
  • Every guess reveals that letter in all three words wherever it appears.
  • Wrong letters add to your wrong count — try to solve the trio with as few wrong guesses as possible!
