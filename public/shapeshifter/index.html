Drag and spin shapes to build a secret picture!

How to Play

  • 🧩 You'll see a faded picture made of shapes.
  • 👆 Drag each shape from the tray onto its matching spot.
  • 🔄 Tap a shape to spin it around until it fits.
  • ✨ When a shape fits, it snaps in place with a happy pop!
  • 🎉 Fill the whole picture to win — then build the next one!
