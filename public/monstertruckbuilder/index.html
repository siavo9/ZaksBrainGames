Monster Truck Builder

Build your own monster truck, one part at a time — solve fun puzzles to unlock each piece!

How to Play

  • 🔧 Your truck starts as a bare frame. Tap a locked part to build it!
  • 🧠 Solve a quick, fun puzzle — counting, science, shapes, or circuits.
  • 🎉 Get it right and the part snaps on with confetti and a happy sound!
  • 🚗 Build every part, then tap Drive! to crush some cars. VROOM!
  • 🌟 No timers, no losing — if a puzzle is tricky, just try again!
