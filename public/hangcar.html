🚗 HangCar

Guess the secret word — one letter at a time! Every wrong guess builds the car. Beat the car!

How to Play

HangCar is a kid-friendly twist on hangman: try to guess the secret word before the car gets fully built!

  • Tap a letter on the keyboard, or press a key on your keyboard.
  • Correct letters appear in the word. Wrong letters add another piece to the car.
  • You have 10 wrong guesses before the car is finished.
  • Solve the word before that to win — click New Word to try another!
