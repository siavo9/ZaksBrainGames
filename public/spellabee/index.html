Spell-a-Bee

Listen to the bee, then tap the letters to spell the word! 🐝

How to Play

  • 🐝 The bee says a word out loud. Tap 🔊 Hear it again any time.
  • 🖼️ A little picture gives you a hint!
  • 👆 Tap the letter tiles in order to fill the boxes.
  • ↩️ Made a mistake? Tap a filled box to take the letter back.
  • 🎉 Spell it right and the bee buzzes with joy — then a new word!
  • 🌟 Not quite? No worries — just fix it and try again!
