How to Play 🚗🛞

Two cars roll up and you count their tires! Add the two numbers together and type your answer. Get it right and watch the tow truck zoom in to haul the cars away!

  • 🔍 Look at the tires on both cars
  • ➕ Add the two tire numbers together
  • ⌨️ Type your answer in the box (or use the number on each car as a hint)
  • 🚀 Hit Submit or press Enter
  • 😊 Tap Easy Mode for smaller numbers
  • 🔊 Tap the speaker to mute or unmute sounds
