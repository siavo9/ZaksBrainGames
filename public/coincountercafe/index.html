Coin Counter Cafe

Run your own cafe! Tap coins to make the exact change for every customer. ☕

How to Play

  • 🐻 A customer orders a yummy snack with a price, like 🍪 Cookie — 7¢.
  • 🪙 Tap coins (1¢, 5¢, 10¢, 25¢) to drop them in the tray.
  • ↩️ Put in too many? Tap a coin in the tray to take it back.
  • ✅ When your money matches the price, tap Pay!
  • 🎉 Get it right and your happy customer cheers — then a new one arrives!
  • 🌟 Not quite? No worries — just keep counting and try again!
