Keepy Uppy
Bop the balloon — don’t let it touch the ground!
1 · Pick your character
2 · Pick a difficulty
3 · How to play
  • 🎈Keepy Uppy is a take-turns game. You and the computer bop a bouncy balloon back and forth — the goal is to keep it off the ground.
  • 🕹️Move with ← →, jump with ↑ or Space. Only your character moves on your turn.
  • 👆Jump so your head bumps the balloon to bop it up. After you hit it, it becomes the other player’s turn.
  • 💥If the balloon touches the ground on your turn, the other player scores a point. Don’t let it drop!
  • ⚡Every 3 hits earns a Boost (up to 3). Press Enter to arm it — your next bop becomes a fiery power shot. Press Enter again to cancel.
  • ⏸️Press P or tap Pause any time. Use the Menu button to come back here.
  • 🏆First to 3 points wins!
Tip: time your jump so the balloon is right above your head.
