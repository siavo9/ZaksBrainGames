How to Play

You're the hero firefighter! Big fires keep popping up around town. Use your trusty fire truck to put them out before time runs out — then a tougher level begins!

  • 👆 Tap anywhere on the screen to shoot water at the nearest fire.
  • ⏱ Put out every fire in 30 seconds to beat the level.
  • ⭐ Each new level adds 5 more fires — how far can you go?
  • 🔊 Tap the speaker button to turn sounds on or off.

Tap the ✕ or anywhere outside to close.
