🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 24, 2026

The short version: Zak's Brain Games is made for kids. It collects no personal information, has no ads, no trackers, and no logins. All the games are built right into the app, so it works fully offline and makes no network requests. Nothing about you ever leaves your device.

Hi! Zak's Brain Games is built to be a safe, friendly place for children to play and learn. This policy explains, in plain language that both kids and grown-ups can understand, exactly what we do (and don't do) with information.

Who this app is for

Zak's Brain Games is intended for children and families. We have designed it to comply with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and to respect the principles of the European Union's data-protection rules for children (GDPR-K). The simplest way we protect kids' privacy is by not collecting any information in the first place.

What information we collect

None. Really, none. We do not collect, store, request, or transmit any personal information. There are:

  • No accounts, sign-ups, or logins
  • No names, email addresses, phone numbers, or birthdays
  • No location data
  • No photos, microphone, or camera access
  • No contact lists or device identifiers
  • No contact or submission forms

No analytics, ads, or trackers

There are no third-party analytics, no advertising, and no tracking technologies anywhere in this app. We do not use cookies to follow you, and we do not build profiles of players. The app does not "phone home."

Works fully offline

Every game is bundled inside the app itself. Once it is installed, Zak's Brain Games runs completely offline and makes no network requests to any outside server. All of the games live inside the app and open in the same window — there are no links out to other websites.

Where game progress is stored

Some games may remember small things like your current streak or best score so the game is more fun to come back to. When they do, that information is saved only on your own device (for example, in the browser's local storage). It is never sent anywhere, never shared, and you can clear it at any time by clearing the app's or browser's data.

No data sharing or selling

Because we collect nothing, there is nothing to share or sell. We do not share, sell, rent, or trade any information with anyone, ever.

No in-app purchases

Zak's Brain Games is free to play. There are no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, and no payment forms anywhere in the app.

For parents and guardians

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects nothing from children under 13 (or any age). There is nothing for a parent to delete, because nothing is ever stored off the device. If you have any questions or concerns about your child's privacy, please reach out — we're happy to help.

Changes to this policy

If we ever update this policy, we will change the date at the top of this page. We will only ever change it to make things clearer or safer.
